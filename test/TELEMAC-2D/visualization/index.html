Method

Inertial wave q^{t+1}=(q^t - g h_flow dt S)/(1+g dt n²|q|/h^{7/3}) (Bates 2010) same as LISFLOOD-FP, approximating TELEMAC-2D Saint-Venant (2D shallow water) on structured grid. Drains: V1 `h[drain]=0.3*h` (70% removal per step → infinite sink). Roads: lower Manning 0.02 vs 0.03. DEM: CartoDEM 30m EGM96 clip 80.155-80.175,13.11-13.13 (5184 cells, min 9.26 max 30.5 mean 16.88).

Full TELEMAC would use unstructured .slf mesh, .cas steering, TELEMAC2D MPI — this prototype validates physics before compilation.
